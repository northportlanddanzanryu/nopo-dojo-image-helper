Privacy Policy: NoPo Dojo Poster

Effective November 8, 2026

NoPo Dojo Poster is an app run by North Portland Danzan Ryu, a jujitsu dojo in Portland, Oregon. It publishes the dojo's own videos to the dojo's own Instagram account and Facebook Page, using Meta's official tools.

What we collect

The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information about followers, visitors, or anyone else. It has no sign-up and no public login.

What the app handles

The app stores the dojo's own videos and captions, and a record of which posts have gone out, including the post IDs from Meta. It uses an access key to reach the dojo's own accounts, kept in encrypted storage.

Purpose

This information is used only to publish the dojo's posts on schedule and to avoid posting the same video twice.

Other services

Posts appear on Instagram and Facebook, which are run by Meta under Meta's own privacy policy. The app runs on GitHub, and this page is hosted by GitHub, which may keep standard visitor logs under its own privacy policy.

Deletion requests

The app holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing for it to delete. If you appear in one of the dojo's posts and want it removed, or have any question, contact us at johnngeth@gmail.com, and we will respond promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
